SessionTuner

Control, Inspect, and Reset Website Sessions.

Clear site data instantly or automatically without switching to Incognito or opening DevTools.

What does it do?

Reset Now

Instantly remove all site-specific data with one click. This includes cookies, Local Storage, Session Storage, Cache Storage, and IndexedDB. Ensuring a completely fresh state for the current website without affecting other tabs or domains.

Auto Clear

Enable automatic cleanup so the moment you close a tab or browser window, all related session data is wiped. This prevents session leakage, stale logins, and tracking persistence. Perfect for testing, shared machines, or privacy-first workflows.

Stats

Quickly see how many keys are a site is using and whether a Service Worker is active without opening DevTools every time.

Config

Fine-tune what gets cleared by selectively excluding storage types like Local Storage, cookies, or IndexedDB. This gives you full control over session resets, allowing you to preserve critical data while still eliminating unwanted state.

Common doubts?

Why SessionTuner over Incognito?

  • Incognito mode starts a fresh session, but you can't inspect, tweak, or partially reset anything. SessionTuner lets you stay in your real browsing context and precisely clear only what matters. without losing logins, extensions, or dev state you want to keep.

Who is this for?

  • Debugging — Fix broken auth, stale cache, or corrupted storage without switching to Incognito.
  • Convenience — Auto-clean sessions on close while keeping extensions and workflows.
  • Control — For devs and testers who want precision, not full resets.
